From scattered activity to a decision system.

The business problem

Before

Plenty of activity. No shared answer.

Outreach notes, emergency calls, viability, sold work and revenue lived in different exports. Account names varied by source and historical records were incomplete. Leadership could see that people were busy, but not reliably connect effort to outcomes.

The question

Is relationship marketing making the phone ring?

The dashboard needed to answer that question without collapsing every downstream issue into “marketing worked” or “sales failed.” Calls, viable opportunities, conversions and revenue each needed their own signal.

How I built it

01Recover history

Consolidated multi-year job and revenue records so older performance stopped disappearing from current reports.

02Resolve identity

Built an alias layer that maps inconsistent referral-source names to one clean account name.

03Align periods

Separated program-to-date activity from current-year calls, conversions and annual revenue.

04Make it actionable

Translated the funnel into plain-language statuses that point to the stage needing attention.

Two systems, two jobs

Priority tier

Where we choose to invest

Forward-looking marketing priority based on opportunity, geography, response capability, relationship strength, historical performance and growth potential.

CRM relationship grade

What the relationship is producing

A current performance designation maintained in the CRM. Keeping it separate from priority prevents a promising target from being mistaken for an already productive client.

The data-quality work

A polished dashboard is only useful if the same account means the same thing in every source.

  • Separated raw names from standardized names.
  • Mapped alternate spellings and department naming conventions.
  • Excluded dispatch-only records from relationship grades.
  • Added both lower and upper date boundaries to annual formulas.
  • Kept “no data” distinct from a genuine zero.
  • Reconciled annual totals against source history before summarizing them.

What changed

Marketing focus became visible

The team can distinguish productive relationships, untouched opportunities and accounts receiving effort without a return.

Funnel friction became specific

Calls, viable jobs and conversions are separate measures, making handoffs easier to discuss without defaulting to blame.

History became usable

Current activity can be viewed beside annual and all-time performance without losing older revenue or mixing reporting periods.

My role

Strategy, data architecture, build and translation
