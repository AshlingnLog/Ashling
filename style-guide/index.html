Ashling.tech.

The design system behind the personal brand of Ashling Struve: creative marketing operations. One idea drives everything — creative warmth × systems precision — and every choice below encodes one side of that pairing or the other.

The idea

Ashling's positioning is "develop the idea, build the campaign, prove it worked" — a creative who builds systems. The brand expresses that duality typographically and chromatically: a warm serif and a marigold spark carry the creative side; petrol teal, monospace labels, and grid motifs carry the operations side. When making any new asset, ask: does this element belong to the warm half or the precise half? Give it that half's tools.

Color

Two palettes, one system. Petrol is the workhorse (links, labels, data); marigold is the spark — spend it sparingly, one accent moment per view. Neutrals are teal-biased, never pure grey. This page renders in whichever theme your device prefers; both palettes are documented below.

One spark per view

Marigold appears once per screen as a deliberate moment — the period after the name, a tag, a border. If it's everywhere, it's nowhere.

Petrol works, marigold plays

Anything functional (links, labels, data callouts) is petrol. Anything expressive (the spark, "also true" flourishes) is marigold.

No pure grey

Every neutral carries a slight teal bias so the whole page feels chosen, not defaulted.

Design both themes

Never ship an asset that only works in light mode. Dark mode lifts petrol to #6FB4B0 and warms marigold — don't naively invert.

Typography

Three faces, three jobs. The pairing itself is the brand: a soft serif (creative) set against a technical mono (systems), with a clean sans doing the everyday work between them. All three are on Google Fonts.

Display · FrauncesWeights 600–700 · tight leading · -0.01em tracking
Builds the structure behind growth.

Headlines, section titles, card titles. The warmth of the brand lives here. Hero scale: clamp(44px, 7vw, 76px). Section scale: 28–38px. Card scale: 20–24px.

Body · Instrument SansWeights 400–600 · 1.6 line-height

Runs marketing as a department of one across three service brands — combining brand storytelling and campaign execution with the CRM administration, automation, and reporting systems that turn a marketing push into measurable pipeline.

All running text at 15–17px. Keep measure near 65 characters. Bold sparingly, for thesis statements only.

Data · Spline Sans MonoWeights 400–500 · uppercase · +0.14em tracking
Analytics · Automation · Built with · Live project

Tags, eyebrows, metadata, contact lines, footers. 12–14px, uppercase, letterspaced. This is the "systems voice" — anything that reads like data gets the mono.

Layout & motifs

Left-aligned, editorial

Content aligns left in a 920–960px container with 24px side padding. Nothing is centered except within components.

Section header + rule

Every section opens with a Fraunces title and a 1px line running to the right margin — the signature structural device.

The dashboard grid

A faint 48px grid (line color at 35% opacity, radially masked) sits behind hero areas only — a quiet nod to the spreadsheet roots.

Cards

10px radius, 1px line border, surface fill, 24–28px padding. Metadata footers separated by a dashed rule.

Tags are true

Mono eyebrow labels (ANALYTICS, AUTOMATION, LIVE PROJECT) classify content honestly — never decorative numbering.

Motion is an entrance

One orchestrated page-load rise (staggered 80ms), nothing scroll-jacked, and everything respects reduced-motion.

Voice

First person, confident, concrete. The voice does the same job as the type pairing: warm delivery, precise content. Puns are welcome when they're earned ("Donut know what you want? Roll for it") — one per piece, never forced.

Sounds like Ashling

"Develop the idea. Build the campaign. Prove it worked."Three-beat thesis statements with periods.

"Sometimes the best marketing fix isn't a campaign."Insight framed as a small twist.

"An idea born the day a tree fell through my house."Real stories, told plainly, with humor intact.

Doesn't

"Results-driven marketing professional leveraging synergies…"No résumé-speak, no leverage, no synergy.

"Passionate about delivering best-in-class solutions!"No exclamation-point enthusiasm; let specifics carry it.

Unverifiable claims and inflated metrics.Every claim on the site maps to something real.
